Device configuration for Nothing Phone (1)

The Nothing Phone (1) (codenamed "Spacewar") is a mid-range smartphone released in 2022 by Nothing.

Basic   | Spec Sheet
-------:|:-------------------------
Platform | Snapdragon® 778G+ (SM7325-AE)
RAM & Storage | 8/128GB, 8GB/256GB, 12GB/256GB, (LPDDR5 RAM, UFS 3.1 storage)
Shipped Android Version | 12
Battery | Non-removable, 4500 mAh

Rear camera | 50MP Main Camera, 50MP Ultra Wide
Front camera | 16MP in-display
